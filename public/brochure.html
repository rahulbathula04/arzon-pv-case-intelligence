SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & FIRST PRINCIPLES

What is Arzon PV Case Intelligence?

An enterprise-grade pharmacovigilance simulation platform bridging academic pharmacology and CRO drug safety operations.

In multinational pharmaceutical corporations, biotechnology sponsors, and global Contract Research Organizations (CROs like IQVIA, Parexel, Syneos, Fortrea, Cognizant, and Accenture), Drug Safety Associates (DSAs) and Pharmacovigilance Specialists are the front line of patient safety. They intake spontaneous reports, clinical trial adverse events, and safety literature, code medical events using MedDRA, assess regulatory seriousness, calculate drug-event causality, and author regulatory CIOMS-I/E2B narratives.

A single misclassified term, overlooked seriousness criterion, or flawed causality evaluation can trigger punitive audit findings from global regulatory bodies (US FDA, EMA, UK MHRA, India CDSCO). Arzon PV Case Intelligence replicates the high-stakes operational workflow of industry safety databases like Oracle Argus Safety and Veeva Vault Safety, training pharmacy (B.Pharm, M.Pharm, Pharm.D), medical (MBBS, BDS), and life sciences graduates into job-ready clinical safety professionals.

EVALUATION RUBRIC

The 4-Pillar Assessment Framework (100 Points Total)

Every submission is scored systematically across four equal clinical pillars:

1. MedDRA Coding Accuracy (25 Pts)

Evaluates exact match against expected Preferred Term (PT) and Primary System Organ Class (SOC). Deducts points for imprecise terms or coding symptoms rather than definitive syndromes.

2. Seriousness Determination (25 Pts)

Benchmarks checked criteria against clinical facts (e.g. hospitalization dates, life-threatening interventions). Validates regulatory justification quality.

3. Causality Assessment (25 Pts)

Evaluates concordance with expected WHO-UMC category and verifies precision of the 10-Question Naranjo score tally and dechallenge logic.

4. CIOMS-I Narrative Quality (25 Pts)

Audits chronological sequence, patient demographics, reaction onset latency, laboratory investigations, corrective action taken, and word count compliance (≥ 40 words).
